About RootPath

How the tracker works, how its content is organised, and the rules it follows.

Purpose

RootPath is a learning tracker for offensive security. It turns labs, CTF rooms and certification goals into one picture: what is done, what is in progress and what comes next, all heading toward a cybersecurity internship.

It is built for self-learners starting out in ethical hacking. The sample data shows a realistic beginner path (TryHackMe and HackTheBox) and is meant to be replaced with your own in js/data.js.

XP, ranks and progress

  • XP is the total of points from labs marked Completed. Each lab's points are set by hand to reflect estimated effort (length and depth), so they follow difficulty loosely but not strictly.
  • Rank is decided by total XP using the thresholds below.
  • Skill radar scores each category as completed points divided by all points available in that category.
  • Certification progress is the share of its linked prep labs that are completed. Certifications with no linked labs (such as Security+) use a manual estimate.

Ethics and scope

  • Practise only on systems you own or have written permission to test, such as TryHackMe, HackTheBox and your own virtual machines.
  • This site teaches no attacks and runs none. It only tracks learning progress.
  • Accounts are simulated for this prototype. Do not enter a real password.
